02

THE METHOD

Four stages, in order, assessed in order. It's also the shape of every BroadVision cafe after you graduate — so you'll live it twice.

GROW
STAGE 01 — WHO YOU ARE

GUIDE

Before you touch a knife, you find out who you are and who Jesus says you are. You get a mentor — one person who picks up the phone, notices you're struggling, and shows up. Most people arrive broken and defensive. This is where that stops.

OUT → A FOUNDATION, AND A PERSON TO CALL AT MIDNIGHT
STAGE 02 — GET COMPETENT

RAISE

Now the boring stuff that quietly wins you a job. English and numbers so the menu maths stops being scary. Serving people without rolling your eyes. Counting stock, writing a prep list, turning up on time when it's raining. This is the stage most people quit in — and the one that decides everything after.

OUT → A SKILL AN EMPLOYER WILL ACTUALLY PAY FOR
STAGE 03 — REAL SHIFTS

OPERATE

Real orders. Real rushes. Real consequences. You work the pass on a live service with customers who don't care that it's your first week. Burn the broth? That's your problem now. Open up, close down, prep for tomorrow, count what you used.

OUT → YOU CAN RUN A SHIFT UNSUPERVISED
STAGE 04 — LEAD

WALK

This is where it flips. You're no longer the person being corrected — you're the one teaching the trainee beside you. Running the shift. Handling a difficult customer. Doing the maths on a new supplier. Watching a younger kid go through exactly what you went through two years ago.

OUT → A TEAM, AND A CAFE OF YOUR OWN
