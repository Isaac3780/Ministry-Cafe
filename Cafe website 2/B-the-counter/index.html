01 — The honest bit

Two things happen at once here. Take one without the other and it falls apart.

You learn a trade

A trade you can be proud of and get paid for. Hotpot sets that come out right every time. A table of eight kept happy. Stock that adds up. Numbers, English, service, admin — and the confidence to walk into a kitchen and not freeze.

You become a disciple

One mentor who actually picks up the phone. A Bible study you don't dread. And a bigger reason to be decent at your job — because how you treat the tired customer at 9pm is part of it.

The cafe isn't a charity project. It's the engine. Every set sold pays for the next trainee's seat.

05 — Where this goes

Train. Graduate. Then give it away.

  1. 1

    Train — 24 months

    Kitchen and floor, books and Bible. English, maths, customer service, cafe operations, inventory, admin, entrepreneurship and leadership. Weekly fellowship is compulsory — yes, really, and nobody's exempt.

    KitchenFloorStockEnglishMathsAdminMoneyLeadingBible
  2. 2

    Graduate — get paid

    Into a restaurant as a chef or a restaurant leader, running a station, then a shift, then a floor. Or open your own small kitchen outright. Either way it's yours.

    ChefRestaurant leaderOwn kitchen
  3. 3

    Multiply — change your town

    Launch a BroadVision cafe partner in your own village, town or region. Jobs for people you grew up with. Your church stronger. And a generation of 19-year-olds who now think a career means something other than leaving.

    Partner cafeChurch partnerNew trainer
