What You Learn

The 18-Month Learning Journey

Six stages, built on five key learning areas — Language, Beverage, Hospitality, Business and Personal Development.

👆👇 Tap a stage to see what you'll learn

01
🔤 Months 1–3

Stage 1 · Basic Learning

Build a strong foundation: language and communication, basic English for daily use, introduction to hospitality, personal discipline and teamwork, basic workplace safety and hygiene.

02
🍲 Months 4–6

Stage 2 · Fundamental Skill Training

Learn the essential skills, hands-on and theory: beverage and drink preparation, food handling and hygiene, customer service, basic computer and cashier skills, communication with customers.

03
📈 Months 7–9

Stage 3 · Practical Skill Development

Strengthen skills through practice: drink preparation and presentation, menu and recipe control, hospitality and serving, stock and ingredient management, basic purchasing and costing.

04
💼 Months 10–12

Stage 4 · Business & Operations

Understand how a business operates: café daily operations, cashiering and sales recording, customer relations, inventory and stock control, basic business administration.

05
🌱 Months 13–15

Stage 5 · Integrated Application

Bring all the skills together: practical beverage training, hospitality and customer service practice, teamwork and operational responsibility, problem-solving, basic entrepreneurship and business planning.

06
🎓 Months 16–18

Stage 6 · Assessment & Development

Prepare for the next step: final practical skill assessments, language and communication assessment, workplace readiness, business knowledge review, individual development and career planning.

Five Key Learning Areas

Learning areaSkills to develop
1. LanguageEnglish communication throughout 18 months
2. BeverageDrinks, preparation and hygiene
3. HospitalityCustomer service, serving and communication
4. BusinessCashiering, stock, costing and operations
5. Personal developmentTeamwork, discipline, confidence and responsibility

All five learning areas are developed progressively throughout the 18 months — language learning continues from Month 1 to Month 18, alongside the other skill areas. The purpose is to teach skills, develop abilities and build confidence, not simply to train people to work in a café.
