Who Is It For?

Basic English? You Can Start Here.

Do you have basic English but want to become more confident in conversation? Are you a young adult, overseas student, or someone preparing to enter the workforce?

Our Livelihood English Programme is designed to help you take the next step. You don't need advanced English to join — if you can understand basic English, you can begin.

  • Young adults preparing for work
  • Overseas students building confidence
  • Anyone wanting everyday conversation skills
  • Lower-income learners — accessible & affordable
