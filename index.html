How Trainees Grow

The G.R.O.W. Model

Every trainee passes through four stages — from faith to founder.

G

Guide

Lead to Christ through mentoring & discipleship. Weekly fellowship compulsory.

R

Raise

Life skills, English, Math, entrepreneurship & leadership.

O

Operate

Hands-on in a real café: chef, floor, inventory, admin.

W

Walk

Walk alongside as they become leaders, disciple-makers, café owners.
